# XPLAY North Star

## Product
XPLAY is a social platform for playable experiences. PLX is the playable format.

A creator should be able to upload media, describe an idea, and receive something that feels like an authored arcade experience—not an engineering prototype.

## Emotional Target
The success reaction is:
**"I can't believe this actually turned my picture / song / idea into this."**

## Product Laws
1. Source-media DNA must be recognizable.
2. The source media must not simply be pasted behind generic gameplay.
3. Every PLX category owns its camera and movement grammar.
4. The Art Department can reject poor extraction.
5. Fallback assets must be visually intentional.
6. AI Generated mode makes creative decisions; Custom mode exposes them.
7. The user sees a creative process, not internal engineering noise.
8. Generated games must be tested before being presented.
9. FPS and Fighting are first-class engines.
10. Never regress working categories to simplify architecture.

## Visual Language
Adult, premium, modern arcade.
Core: white, dark navy/near-black, teal/cyan.
Controlled neon accents.
Avoid kiddie UI, gray-box developer screens, and generic geometric gameplay art.

## Strategic Architecture
XPLAY behaves like a miniature AI game studio:
Calibrator → Executive Director → Specialist Game Director → Casting → Art Department → Level Director → Compiler → Critic → Repair → Runtime.
