# XPLAY V10 Arcade Ascension Brief

## V10 Theme
**Arcade Ascension**

V9 made the system more truthful and functional.
V10 makes it more convincing and desirable.

## Outcome Target
A user browsing the site should instantly feel that each PLX could plausibly exist as an indie arcade title.

## Required Upgrades
1. Stronger card imagery.
2. Runtime-captured or runtime-faithful showcase frames.
3. Category-specific casts.
4. Better visual-energy in scenes.
5. Higher parity between “what the tile promises” and “what play delivers.”

## North Star
Move XPLAY away from:
- generic placeholders,
- sparse scenes,
- mascot-in-a-world cards,
- abstraction that reads as dev scaffolding.

Move XPLAY toward:
- convincing frames,
- genre-correct readability,
- indie-arcade polish,
- stronger player fantasy.
