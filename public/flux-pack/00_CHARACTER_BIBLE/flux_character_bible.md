# Flux Character Bible

## Core Identity
Flux is a chameleon who can enter any playable world and adapt to its rules, style, and environment.

## Non-negotiables
- Curled chameleon tail
- Big expressive eyes
- Teal body with navy details
- X chest insignia
- Agile, parkour-capable silhouette
- Visually readable even when simplified

## Expression Range
Calm, curious, focused, playful, stunned, determined, victorious, hidden/camouflaged.

## Usage Notes
Use Classic Flux as the canonical reference. All future style variants should preserve the same recognizable silhouette.
